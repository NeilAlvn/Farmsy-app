Hey — deep links are broken, and it's a web-side fix. Real impact: this is the referral capture path, so right now referrals silently don't get credited.

The flow is meant to be: someone taps farmsy.app/join?ref=CODE with the app installed -> OS opens the app -> app grabs the ref code, holds it, and sends it on signup so the referrer gets credited (mirrors the web's 7-day farmsy_ref cookie). Because the .well-known files are empty, the OS won't verify the app, so /join?ref=CODE just opens the browser, the app never sees the code, and the referrer is never credited. Fails silently — worst kind.

The two files are live but EMPTY:
  /.well-known/assetlinks.json                -> []
  /.well-known/apple-app-site-association      -> {"applinks":{"details":[]}}

The app side and the /join page are both fine. Just need these files filled in, on BOTH farmsy.app and www.farmsy.app.

Serving rules that trip people up:
- Content-Type: application/json
- apple-app-site-association has NO .json extension
- NO redirect — Apple follows no redirects for the AASA, a 301 silently breaks it
- Must be reachable at exactly /.well-known/... on both the apex and www
